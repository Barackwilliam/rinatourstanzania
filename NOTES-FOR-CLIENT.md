# Content still needed from Rina Tours

all 174 tours are loaded — these are the gaps that stop tours from being
sellable, in the order worth chasing.

whether park fees, meals and accommodation are in the price.

- **Nature Tours** — all 25

## 2. Day-by-day itineraries

Around half the multi-day tours have no daily breakdown. A seven-day trip
listed with no day structure is hard to sell against a competitor who shows
theirs. Notably: 7 Days Great Migration Safari, 7 Days Luxury Northern
Circuit, both Nyerere boat safaris, both Ruaha tours.

## 3. "Best for" — 85 tours

Short line on who each trip suits. Quick to write and it helps visitors
self-select.

## 4. Category description — Safari / Wildlife Tours

The other six categories each have one; this one does not.

## 5. Photographs

No images were supplied. The site needs, at minimum:
- One landscape shot per tour (174), or at least one per category to start
- One per destination (55 places)
- 3-5 hero images or short clips for the homepage
- Team photos if the About page is to show the team

Video for the homepage hero: H.264 mp4, 1080p, no sound, 8-15 seconds, under
6 MB. Raw phone video will be far too heavy for mobile data.

## 7. Worth a decision, not a correction

Several experiences appear in more than one category at different prices:

- Usambara Mountains — trekking, nature and camping, all USD 350
- Udzungwa Mountains — trekking 350, nature 350, camping 450
- Sanje Waterfall — trekking 100, nature 100, camping 250
- Empakai Crater — trekking and nature, both USD 150, both 1 day

Where the price and duration are identical, it is likely one product listed
twice. The site can show a single tour under several categories, so these can
be merged if that is what they are. Confirm which are genuinely different
trips and which are duplicates.
