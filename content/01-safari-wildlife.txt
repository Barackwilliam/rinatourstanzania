Short Description:
Escape the busy streets of Dar es Salaam and experience the beauty of Tanzania's wildlife on a full-day safari to Mikumi National Park. This package is ideal for travellers with limited time who still want to experience an authentic African safari.

Full Description:
Your adventure begins with an early morning departure from Dar es Salaam towards Mikumi National Park. As you approach the park, the landscape changes from urban surroundings to beautiful African savannah.

Once inside the park, enjoy a full-day game drive through the wilderness with opportunities to see elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, wildebeest, hippos, antelopes and, with some luck, lions and other predators.

The safari is accompanied by a professional guide who will help you understand the animals, their behaviour and the surrounding ecosystem. You will also have plenty of opportunities to take photographs and enjoy Tanzania's spectacular natural scenery.

At the end of the day, you will begin your journey back to Dar es Salaam.
