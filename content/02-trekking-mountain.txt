PACKAGE 01
MOUNT KILIMANJARO – MARANGU ROUTE
==================================================

Category:
Trekking / Mountain Climbing

Duration:
6 Days / 5 Nights

Starting Point:
Moshi / Arusha

Destination:
Mount Kilimanjaro

Route:
Marangu Route

Price:
From USD 1,500 per person

Short Description:
Climb Africa's highest mountain through the scenic Marangu Route and experience an unforgettable journey from tropical rainforest to the spectacular summit of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Full Description:
The Marangu Route is one of the most popular routes for climbing Mount Kilimanjaro. The route offers a gradual approach to the mountain and is known for providing hut accommodation rather than camping.

Your journey begins in the lower rainforest zone before gradually ascending through different ecological zones. As you climb higher, the landscape changes from lush forest to moorland, alpine desert and finally the high-altitude summit zone.

The final summit attempt begins during the night so that climbers can reach Uhuru Peak around sunrise, weather and conditions permitting.

The experience is supported by professional mountain guides and a dedicated mountain crew throughout the climb.

Highlights:
- Mount Kilimanjaro
- Uhuru Peak
- Marangu Route
- Kilimanjaro National Park
- Rainforest
- Moorland
- Alpine desert
- Summit sunrise
- Mountain scenery
- Professional mountain guides
- Mountain crew
- Wildlife and nature experience

Typical Itinerary:

Day 1:
Marangu Gate to Mandara Hut

Day 2:
Mandara Hut to Horombo Hut

Day 3:
Horombo Hut to Kibo Hut

Day 4:
Kibo Hut to Uhuru Peak and Horombo Hut

Day 5:
Horombo Hut to Marangu Gate

Day 6:
Departure / transfer

Includes:
- Kilimanjaro National Park fees
- Mountain guide
- Mountain crew
- Hut accommodation
- Meals during the climb
- Drinking water
- Park permits
- Rescue arrangements according to park regulations
- Transfers according to itinerary

Excludes:
- International flights
- Tanzania visa
- Travel insurance
- Personal climbing equipment
- Tips for mountain crew
- Personal expenses
- Accommodation before and after the climb unless specified
- Items not mentioned in the inclusions

Best For:
First-time mountain climbers, adventure travellers, couples, groups and travellers looking for a classic Kilimanjaro experience.
