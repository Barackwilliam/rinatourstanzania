PACKAGE 01
ARUSHA NATIONAL PARK NATURE TOUR
==================================================

Category:
Nature Tours

Duration:
1 Day

Starting Point:
Arusha

Destination:
Arusha National Park

Price:
From USD 100 per person

Short Description:
Discover the beautiful landscapes, forests, lakes and wildlife of Arusha National Park on a refreshing full-day nature experience.

Full Description:
Arusha National Park is one of Tanzania's most accessible natural destinations and offers a beautiful combination of forests, grasslands, lakes and mountain scenery.

The park is home to giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, warthogs, monkeys and many bird species. Visitors can enjoy game viewing while experiencing the peaceful natural environment close to Arusha.

Highlights:
- Arusha National Park
- Mount Meru views
- Momella Lakes
- Wildlife
- Forests
- Bird watching
- Nature photography
- Scenic landscapes

Best For:
Families, couples, photographers, bird lovers and first-time visitors.
